# Surface brief: Ben-Day (game decks and home page)

THESIS: hack.party as a 1970s Saturday-night game show, printed as a pop-art comic in four
process inks.

OWN-WORLD: Lichtenstein-era comics: Ben-Day dots, fat black panel lines, yellow caption
boxes, speech balloons and sound-effect starbursts. Cream newsprint, red, yellow, blue and
black. Dots stay large and pale on the backdrop only, so the Zoom feed never moirés them.

STORY: "Meanwhile, in Studio 7..." The show opens on a caption box, every slide prints in
through a halftone screen, panels land with a comic overshoot, and every verdict is a
KAPOW! on a starburst.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a white panel with the Shrikhand wordmark (yellow, black keyline, red print
offset) under a caption box; the home page opens on the same wordmark and the next game in
a yellow panel.

FORM: seed: none. Code-first build; no image comps.

FINISH: sized for a 4K window sent over Zoom at 1080p; text passes 4.5:1; every animation
has a reduced-motion path; Classic untouched.
