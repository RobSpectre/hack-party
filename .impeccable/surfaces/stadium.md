# Surface brief: Stadium (game decks and home page)

THESIS: hack.party as the summer games of 1972, covered by a public broadcaster whose
graphics desk was trained in Swiss design.

OWN-WORLD: the 1970s games identity tradition: one grotesk in sentence case, flat fields of
sky, grass and orange, and a ribbon of six games colors as the only ornament. No shadows,
no rounding, no texture. Results are medals.

STORY: competitors are announced, every slide is handed over by a sky-and-grass band that
drops down the screen, panels run in from their lane, and every round ends at the podium:
a gold medal on the ribbon, or an empty ring.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a white card with the ribbon on top and a heavy lower-case wordmark; the
home page opens on the wordmark and the next game on a ribbon-topped card, listed like the
day's programme.

FORM: seed: none. Code-first build; no image comps.

FINISH: sized for a 4K window sent over Zoom at 1080p; text passes 4.5:1; every animation
has a reduced-motion path; Classic untouched.
