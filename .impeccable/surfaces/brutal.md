# Surface brief: Brutal (game decks and home page)

THESIS: hack.party at full volume, with nothing to hide: a game show built from paper,
black ink and two flat colors, where every element is a slab that lands with a thud.

OWN-WORLD: neobrutalist web design crossed with hand-pasted game-show cue cards. Raw paper,
5px black strokes, hard slab shadows, Archivo Black shouting in capitals. Nothing glows,
blurs or eases gently.

STORY: the show is a stack of slabs. Each slide cuts in behind a yellow slab in seven hard
steps, panels slam into place, and the verdict floods the whole screen yellow (or pink).

FIRST VIEWPORT: a white title slab with HACK.PARTY in black capitals over the opening
video; the home page opens on the wordmark and the next game in a yellow slab.

FORM: seed: none. Code-first build; no image comps.

FINISH: sized for a 4K window sent over Zoom at 1080p; text passes 4.5:1; every animation
has a reduced-motion path; Classic untouched.
