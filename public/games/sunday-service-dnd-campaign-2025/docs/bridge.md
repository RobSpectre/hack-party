turbolift, it is evident that Serious Business is afoot in this place. The room
is small but well appointed with panels at every corner. Each member on this
ship's bridge keeps their eyes locked on their console, though their body
language stiffens upon the entry.

A young man with a yellow shirt approaches. "Captain. The con is yours."
