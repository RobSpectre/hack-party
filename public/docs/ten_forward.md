# Ten Forward

## Setting

Light jazz plays over a hushed din of polite conversation. The space around you
bustles with the activity of colleagues, acquaintances and romances
flourishing. There are no cords of wood, no hearth, no mugs, no spit, no simmering pot.
However, there is ample food, tall glasses filled with social drinks of
multiple hues. There are surfaces that appear to be hard with compound
formations of a material you cannot recognize.

And in portals as tall as the 30 foot ceiling across the length of the room
there are shooting stars it what appears to be some galactic display.

This is a tavern. But one unlike you've ever seen before.

A man dressed similarly with a blue top walks by. "Good evening Captain van
Helsing. Sirs."
