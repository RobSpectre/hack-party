# Hive Core

## Setting

You arrive in the heart of the borg cube. Catwalks spider around the huge
cavity of the ship's warp core. You can feel the structure straining as the
warp core perturbs space/time at its absolute limit in the pursuit of speed to
reach Terra before their intruders succeed. A bright green flashes from the
warp core - it's eerie reflection flashes over the occasional surfaces of the
chamber, across the rare chrome reflection that remain from centuries of
assimilation.

In the center of the chamber, a large empty conduit pad stands.
